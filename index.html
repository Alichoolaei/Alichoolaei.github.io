About

Ali Choolaei is an Iranian musician and composer based in Germany who enjoys improvising and exploring new sounds. He plays traditional Persian instruments such as the setar, the tar and the kamancheh, and also works with electronics and sound design.

He also composes music for films and experiments with new approaches to music and sound installations.

Sound Installation

Singing Ulmus

Sound installation. Prager Platz, Berlin.

A trunk of an American elm (Ulmus americana), carbonized and sealed with tung oil, becomes an instrument. A piezo sensor inside the wood is connected to a Raspberry Pi. When someone knocks on the trunk, the Pi reads the sensor and the tree answers with sound.

Materials: carbonized American elm, tung oil, piezo sensor, Raspberry Pi.
